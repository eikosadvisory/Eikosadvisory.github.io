How engagements work

Three shapes, and where to start

Fixed fee for the work. A success element only where the outcome is defined and verifiable.

Diagnostic

Fixed fee, short
  • A tender, supply agreement, offer or draft specification read, with a written opinion
  • Delivered as findings, not as a proposal for more work

Defined scope

Fixed fee in milestones, plus a fee on the outcome where one is defined
  • A bid, an appraisal, a certification programme, a supplier introduction to a bid
  • Fee on the order placed or closure achieved; none in bid advisory or assurance

Retained

Monthly retainer, or per call-off; fee on orders won where applicable
  • A programme carried through: a bid season, an entry route, a manufacturer’s sales support
  • Retainer set off against any fee on orders

Independence

Stated in advance, because the question will be asked

Cannot be combined on one project

  • Bid advisory to a developer and lender’s appraisal of that same project
  • Drafting a regulation, order or tender document for an authority and advising a party on its response to that same document
  • Advice to a buyer on supplier selection and an undisclosed fee from a supplier under consideration
  • Expert opinion for one party and any prior engagement for the other on the same facts

How it is handled

  • A conflict check before any engagement is accepted, on the project and on the counterparties
  • One side of a project at a time, declared in writing
  • Any fee payable by a third party is disclosed in writing to the client and accepted before it is earned, and is never taken in bid advisory or on an assurance mandate
  • The LLP’s registered objects bar it from manufacturing, supplying, installing, operating or trading in any plant or equipment of a kind it audits, inspects or reports upon
  • No remuneration is linked to the outcome or the findings of any audit
  • Prior work disclosed to a tribunal or a lender without being asked
An opinion is worth what its independence is worth. A practice that will take either side of the same question is selling advocacy, and it should be priced as advocacy.
